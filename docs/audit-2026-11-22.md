## 3. Why no recording shows a stable balance

1. **The pitch loop was commanded below its stability floor.** Stability requires
   `kp ≥ A1/A2`. With the corrected plant that is 5.39 Nm/rad and `kp_base=21.57`,
   i.e. **`pscale ≥ 0.25`**. Every armed run in the logs used `pscale 0.10–0.20`;
   the 2026-11-20 `stat` line reads `kpnm=2.9 kdnm=0.7` at `pscale=0.10`.
2. **The 2026-11-20 fall is the signature of an unstable loop, not a sign error.**
   In `jbot-telemetry-1795191006.csv` the body swings from −20.7° to +28.8° in
   about 0.2 s, pitch rate reaches 419 dps, hall speeds ±140 rpm, while the
   applied common-mode torque stays within ±289 units; `mode=4` latches at
   28.75° pose error.
3. **The 2026-11-22 firmware starts at `pscale=1.0`** (`state.rs:192`). The first
   `arm` in session `1795364088238` was accepted, and the next `stat` 397 ms later
   already reported `stop=pose_error`. No telemetry recording covers that attempt.
4. **Commands accepted but not applied in the committed firmware:** `speed` was
   stored and telemetried but never entered `BalanceInput`; `yaw_kp_units` was 0.
   Both are wired up in the uncommitted work tree together with a `pscale`-like
   runtime torque-ramp knob (`slew`, 100…10000 units/s). None of that is flashed;
   `stat` still has no `slew` field.
5. **Older recordings are confounded** by the pre-2026-11-19 negated mixer, which
   sent common-mode torque to the `steer` channel (pivot in place). See
   `docs/design_balance_home.md` §8.3–§8.7.

Claimed loop rate from telemetry is 199–200 Hz and `imu_fresh=1`; there is no
logic-analyzer capture of GP10 in the repository, so jitter and command-to-torque
latency remain unmeasured. `imu_err=1, imu_code=1` is the latched last I2C error,
not proof of a current fault (`stats reset` clears it).

## 5. Plan, in approval order

**Phase 0 — approvals.** Obtain the §0.2 decision for any change to the 200 Hz
loop, fail-safe, limits, protocol, or timeouts. Fix the operating envelope:
rope + hands, movement ≤0.5 m, e-stop reachable. Note that the firmware's 3 m
`RANGE_LIMIT_M` is **not** a substitute for the cable limit.

**Phase 1 — measure, do not tune.** Verify the flashed `TRQ_MODE`; measure N·m
per 1000 units and the drive dead zone on blocks; confirm the wheel-speed and
`test` signs; measure the real serial timeouts and the GP10 jitter/latency.
Acceptance: torque scale within ±20 % of the assumed 5 Nm, dead-zone bracket
recorded, sign checks reproduced, latency artifacts stored.

**Phase 2 — bring the written code into the image.** Format/check/test/clippy,
add `slew` to `stat` and telemetry, then flash. Acceptance: `stat` reports
`kpnm=21.6 kdnm=5.9`, `slew` is accepted and visible, arm refusals still work.

**Phase 3 — first stable balance.** `slew 100…300`, `pscale 0.25`, robot roped,
wheels off the floor at start. `arm` at ≤5° pose error. Acceptance: 10 s hold with
|pitch| < 2°, |x| < 0.1 m, no saturation, `stop=none`; then a small push and a
0.25 → 0.30 → 0.40 → 0.60 ladder, each step scored with `jbot-analyze`
(`oscillation`, `law_fit`, `breakaway`). Stop and latch on any guard.

**Phase 4 — auto-tuning.** Offline first: derive `units_per_nm`, dead zone, and
command-to-rpm delay from telemetry, then replace `PlantParams::NOMINAL` and
`config/robot.toml` with measured values so `Tuning::from_plant` re-derives all
gains. Only after that, and only with a separate approval, run a bounded,
supervised `pscale` search (0.25…1.0) with 10 s windows and a score from existing
telemetry fields (pitch RMS, settling time, saturation fraction, travel, fall
count). The search must never weaken fall guards or the e-stop. There is no
self-tuning adaptive controller in the 200 Hz path.

**Phase 5 — acceptance.** ≥10 min hold (M3-25), push recovery, 4°/6° slope, fall
latch + explicit re-arm, link loss, e-stop from any mode, watchdog reset, and the
full Rust quality-gate command set.

## 6. Phase 1 execution notes (2026-11-22)

**Live link check (read-only commands only).** At `up=687 s` the console reported
`fb=0, fb_ok=0, bat=0.00` while `rx=1,086,384` and `fb_drop=1,086,367` — bytes
were arriving on UART0 but none formed a valid 18-byte frame. Roughly 24 s later
the same boot reported `fb=1839`, `fb_ok=1`, with `fb_drop` no longer growing and
about 124 frames/s arriving. This is the `rx > 0, fb = 0` failure class from
`docs/design_balance_home.md` §8.1; its cause (mainboard power cycle or a stale
profile on the left cable) was not identified from the console alone. Current
baseline: `loop=200`, `pscale=1.00`, `kpnm=21.6`, `mount=0`, `nvm=1`, `estop=0`,
`armed=0`, `zero` and `level` not captured this session.

**Safety gate before any torque:** the robot must be on blocks, secured by the
rope, with hands on the body and the e-stop reachable. Do not send `arm`.

**measurement checklist**

1. `stats reset`, then `stat` — save the baseline counters.
2. `stream 50` and start the console CSV recorder before any `test`.
3. Sign checks (disarmed, e-stop clear): `test 50 50` must roll forward with
   `L ≈ +7…9`, `R ≈ −7…−10` rpm; `test 100 -100` must pivot in place with
   `gz ≈ −30 dps`. Send `test 0` between attempts.
4. Torque scale: with the body held on blocks, step `test 20/40/60/80/100` and
   measure the wheel force at `r = 0.0825 m`. Record N·m per 1000 units and
   compare with the assumed 5 Nm total (200 units/Nm).
5. Dead zone: increase the request in small steps and record the first value at
   which the hall speed leaves zero; this is the dead-zone bracket.
6. Serial timeout: disconnect the Pico-mainboard cable and measure how long the
   wheels keep driving. Target from `M3-38` is 100 ms; the current source sets
   `SERIAL_TIMEOUT=160` (~800 ms).
7. Jitter/latency: capture GP10 with a logic analyzer and correlate the command
   with the hall response. Store the capture next to the CSV.
8. Stop recording and run `jbot-analyze` on the session; keep the raw files.

**Results of the first run** (raw file `jbot-phase1-raw-1795373469.log`):

* Link healthy during the run: `fb_ok=1`, feedback age 5 ms, `bat=39.70 V`,
  `loop=199…200`, `armed=0`, `stop=none`. `stats reset` cleared the latched
  `imu_err=1` to 0; no new I2C error appeared.
* `test 50 50`: applied spin torques ramp 12 → 50 units on both wheels and hall
  speeds go 0 → +341 / −336 rpm. The forward-roll sign is confirmed.
* `test 100 -100`: applied torques are +100 / −100 as requested, but the held
  body keeps `gz` within ±2 dps, so the yaw sign cannot be confirmed with the
  operator holding the robot. Repeat free-standing on the floor before trusting
  the yaw path.
* Ladder 20/40/60/80/100 units: the wheels already turn at 20 units when
  unloaded, and hall speed reaches the ±400 rpm command limit by 60 units. This
  setup measures the speed cap, not torque: **the torque scale and the loaded
  dead zone remain unmeasured**. The only existing dead-zone estimate is the
  historical 84…284-unit bracket from session `1795045179` (§8.5).
* The run ended with `estop` latched; `clear` is required before further tests.

**Automated torque-scale attempt (no external gauge).** The host tool
`tools/pico/jbot_torque_id.py` applies limited spin-test torques with the wheels
hanging free and derives `M = I * alpha` from the hall feedback, using the
per-wheel inertia `0.0078 kg m^2` from `config/robot.toml`. With the operator
holding the body still (pitch range <1 deg), the 40-unit step gave 0.108–0.114 Nm
per wheel, i.e. **175–185 units/Nm total** against the 200 units/Nm assumption
(within 8–12 %). The 30-unit step was biased down by stiction and the 50/60/80
steps were biased up by body compliance, so only the mid-range point is used.
The loaded breakaway ramp showed no wheel motion at 80 and 120 units, i.e. the
loaded dead zone is above 120 units, consistent with the historical 84–284-unit
bracket. The tool ends with `estop`; `clear` is required before more tests.

**Serial timeout (operator stopwatch, approximate).** With `test 100 100`
applied and both wheels free, unplugging the left cable stopped the wheels in
about 2 s as reported by the operator. That includes mechanical coast-down; the
source timeout is `160 x 5 ms = 800 ms`. The 100 ms target from `M3-38` is not
met. A precise number needs a logic analyzer or a mainboard-side probe on PA2.

**Mainboard sleep.** After several minutes idle the console stops answering and
telemetry disappears. This is `INACTIVITY_TIMEOUT = 8 min` (`config.h:186`) with
the counter reset only when `|cmdL| > 50 || |cmdR| > 50` (`main.c:600-605`);
`poweroff()` then removes power from the Pico. Recovery: hold the mainboard
power button 2–3 s until the beep, then reconnect USB if the Pico does not
enumerate. Keep bench commands above 50 units between long measurement pauses.

### Phase 3 first attempts (2026-11-22)

* Both nodes were rebuilt and flashed: F103 with `make -e VARIANT=VARIANT_USART`
  and `make flash` (the mainboard power button must be held during the write or
  the MCU releases the latch), Pico with `cargo run --release` from
  `firmware/pico2w` after BOOTSEL. `stat` then showed `slew=3000`, `fb_ok=1`.
* Safe profile applied at runtime: `pscale 0.30` (`kpnm=6.5`, `kdnm=1.8`),
  `slew 100`. Zero was captured at `pitch=-2.81, roll=2.25` and level at `-0.19`.
* Attempt 1 (operator holds the body, 10 s): `mode=2` for all 474 samples,
  `stop=none`, no fall; torque stayed within ±9 units and the wheels did not
  move. This proves the law is armed and calm at equilibrium, but it is not a
  recovery test.
* Attempt 2 (push shortly after `arm`, 15 s): the body swung from +3° to about
  −18°, the wheels reached ±35 rpm and torque ±66 units, then the 30° guard
  latched `stop=pose_error`, `fall=1` and zeroed the command. No auto-rearm
  occurred. The fall path behaved as designed; the low-gain profile could not
  recover that lean. Raw files: `jbot-push-test2-1795375795.log`.

Two more manual pushes were attempted at the same profile. In both cases the
operator reported a 2–3° nudge, but telemetry shows a jump from about −2° to
+20…+31° in roughly 0.4 s with gyro rates above 100 dps, then the 30° guard
latched `pose_error` (`fall` counter reached 3). Applied torque stayed within
±58 units, so the law did command a correction but could not arrest that
impulse. Raw files: `jbot-push-test2-1795375795.log`,
`jbot-push-gentle-1795375854.log`, `jbot-push-final-1795375921.log`.

Conclusion: a hand-applied disturbance is not reproducible on this bench — the
body leaves the operator's hand in a step rather than a 2–3° lean. Further
manual pushes at this gain add no evidence. The next disturbance must be
command-driven (`home 3` with the existing 10 deg/s acceleration-limited
reference, then `home 0`) or the gain must be raised in a controlled step, both
with a fresh approval. The last test left `estop` latched on purpose; `clear`
is required before another attempt.

## 7. Do not

- Do not raise `I_MOT_MAX` above 10 A without MOSFET and pack temperature data.
- Do not start with `pscale=1.0` or `slew=3000`; those are compiled defaults, not
  a soft start.
- Do not apply `firmware/stm32f103/eferu-jbot.patch` verbatim: it predates the
  `TRQ_MODE` change in the submodule diff.
- Do not use the untracked `crates/jbot_analysis/src/balancer_model.rs` for
  limits: it assumes 2.3 kg wheels, 2.5 Nm total torque and `m·g·L·sin(pitch)`,
  giving an 11.75° limit instead of the plant model's 53.1°.
- Do not treat `stats` from a sub-floor `pscale` run as evidence about the
  machine's recovery margin — the loop was unstable by construction.
